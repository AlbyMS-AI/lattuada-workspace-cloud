## Testo

Three operators now control more than half of the Italian iGaming market.

Since 13 November 2025, more than 350 skin licences disappeared. Forty-six operators remain, holding fifty-two concessions between them. The story everyone tells is simple: fewer players, tighter rules, a tidier market.

It is accurate. It is also incomplete.

Skins let a single concessionaire run ten different front-ends for ten different audiences. That lever is gone. Every one of the 46 operators now competes through the same channels: SEO, affiliation, app, retail. Nobody has an extra front-end to hide behind.

So where does the competition move? To the one thing regulation never touched: the product. Slot depth, live experience, sportsbook pricing. And to the one asset the reform accidentally created: certified player data through SPID and CIE, building a behavioural database smaller competitors cannot replicate quickly.

For affiliates, the multi-brand playbook is over too. Fewer skins mean fewer placements, and the ones left need to work harder per operator.

If your product roadmap for the next eighteen months still assumes the pre-November market, you are planning for a market that no longer exists.

What changed for you since the skins disappeared? Save this if you are mapping your roadmap against the new field.
