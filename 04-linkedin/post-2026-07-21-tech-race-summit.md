## Testo pubblicato

Uptime becomes a headline the moment it fails, never before.

That is the paradox Tech Race Summit 2026 puts on stage on 10 September in Warsaw: engineers from Google, AWS, Oracle, Cloudflare, Fastly and Gcore, plus a keynote from Andrey Doronichev, talking about the systems nobody notices until they break.

Fifteen years in iGaming means fifteen years of traffic spikes with zero warning: a match going to penalties, a jackpot hitting, a promo dropping at the wrong hour. The infrastructure decisions behind those moments rarely make it into a case study, but they decide whether the product survives its own success.

AI is entering that same layer now, not as a headline feature but as another system that has to hold under load. Nobody wants to be the case study for what happens when it does not.

That is the ground #TechRaceSummit is built on: high-load systems, AI in engineering, reliability, and the people who keep all three running when it matters most.
