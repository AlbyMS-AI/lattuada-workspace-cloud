## Testo

Fifteen years in iGaming, and sportsbook still surprised me.

I just completed the SOFTSWISS Sportsbook Essentials course. Not because sportsbook was new territory — I've been in the industry long enough to know the basics — but because understanding a product from the inside changes how you talk about it.

What stood out: the margin logic is entirely different from casino. In slots, the house edge is fixed and embedded. In sports betting, margins shift with liquidity, odds management, and player profiling. The conversation with operators changes completely when you understand what they are actually managing.

For someone working on the Game Aggregator side, knowing how sportsbook economics work is not a theoretical exercise. Operators running both verticals see their portfolio differently. So should the people they work with.

One course does not make you an expert. But it sharpens the right questions.
