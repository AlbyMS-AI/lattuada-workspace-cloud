## Testo del post di lancio (EN)

Ask an operator who creates value in betting and aggregators rarely make the list.

The value we create doesn't show up as a line item. It shows up as costs nobody had to pay.

A single certification report for one provider, in one regulated market, is real money. Multiply that by a full catalogue, and by every market an operator wants to enter, and compliance becomes the real barrier. Not integration. Not content.

Operators feel this as timeline risk long before they touch an API: contract windows built around someone else's certification process, penalty clauses that don't distinguish between their delay and a supplier's.

Spread that same cost across a catalogue already certified once, and the price per game drops fast.

That drop happens because someone absorbs the complexity, so operators, providers, and affiliates stop negotiating it bilaterally every time.

Aggregating games was never the hard part. Aggregating risk is.
