## Slide
**1 — Hook**
Five regulated iGaming markets in Europe. The same six months, five different moments.
Where Italy, Germany, Belgium, the Czech Republic and Ireland sit in the regulatory cycle, and the next date to watch in each.

**2 — What stays the same, what changes**
In all five you need a licence. In all five you read the same four layers: what you can offer, to whom and up to what limit, what tax leaves of the margin, who you can work with.
What changes is the phase each market is in. No licence tells you that, and it decides what comes next.

**3 — Ireland · Opening**
Remote betting licences have been live since 1 July 2026. In-person betting follows on 1 December. Online casino and lotteries come later, with no date set yet.
In an opening market, the licence for your vertical may not exist yet.

**4 — Germany · Review**
The 2021 State Treaty requires a full evaluation report by 31 December 2026, looking first at the unlicensed market and at protection for players with gambling problems. A second targeted amendment was approved in 2025, before the report.
In a review phase, the questions the law asks itself tell you where to look.

**5 — Italy · Implementation**
The online half of the reform is in force: new remote concessions since 13 November 2025, and a €100 weekly cap on cash top-ups at retail top-up points since 13 May 2026. The land-based half has stalled. The delegation to rewrite it expired on 29 August 2026 without a decree, and current concessions run to the end of 2026.
Half a reform is read from the half that is missing.

**6 — Belgium · Tightening**
General advertising ban from July 2023. Minimum age 21 from September 2024. No gambling advertising in stadiums from January 2025. Shirt sponsorship for top-division clubs ends on 1 January 2028. In 2026, blocking orders reach internet intermediaries too.
Strict, but written into the calendar years ahead.

**7 — Czech Republic · Stability**
The Gambling Act (186/2016) has seen no major change in 2026. The last lever to move was tax: from January 2024 the lower rate rose from 23% to 30%, covering sports betting, live games and bingo. The 35% on lotteries and technical games stayed.
When the gambling rules stand still, the budget can still move your margin.
