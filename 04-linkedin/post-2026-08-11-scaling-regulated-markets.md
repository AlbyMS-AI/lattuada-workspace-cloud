## Testo (commentario sopra il repost)

A regulated-market growth playbook breaks in the same place, every time: licensing.

That's exactly the conversation SOFTSWISS is opening up on August 27, and it's worth being in the room for. Partnerships, localisation, go-to-market sequencing get plenty of airtime. What actually sets the timeline gets less.

In my own BDM work, the technical integration is rarely the bottleneck. A provider can sit ready to go live for months while the license moves through a regulator on its own timeline.

Licensing doesn't scale the way commercial strategy does, because each regulated market is a different product, not a different form to fill in. Italy requires RNG certification and branded reports for every title. Germany caps stakes at one euro per spin, forces a five-second wait between rounds, and bans autoplay and jackpots outright. Belgium doesn't cap bonuses. It bans them.

Three regulated markets, three different products to build. That's the layer I'm hoping the panel gets into.

What does carry over: compliance built for a stricter market often clears the bar for a lighter one. Knowing which certification travels and which one you start over on is half the job of growing into multiple jurisdictions.
